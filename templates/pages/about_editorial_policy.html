{% if is_gujarati %} અમારી સંપાદકીય ટીમ કોઈપણ કાનૂની દાવો પ્રકાશિત કરતા પહેલા નીચેના સ્ત્રોત ક્રમને અનુસરે છે: {% else %} Every substantive legal assertion on this platform adheres to a strict four-tier evidence hierarchy: {% endif %}

  1. Tier 1 — Primary Statutory Instruments: Official Assented Act, Extraordinary Gazette issues, and Gazette commencement notifications.
  2. Tier 2 — Subordinate Legislation: Rules, statutory forms, and official administrative circulars issued under statutory authority.
  3. Tier 3 — Committee Material: Official Gujarat UCC Committee reports, working papers, and assembly tablings.
  4. Tier 4 — Secondary Legal Reporting: Cross-checked mainstream news reporting used only for timeline corroboration, never as a substitute for statutory text.
