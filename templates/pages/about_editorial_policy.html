Outdated implementation status Monitor official Gazette and government sources; assign a status owner; show “last verified” date on every legal explainer.
Legal misinformation / ambiguity Qualified reviewer, source-level citations, review workflow, corrections log and clear uncertainty labels. Never infer status.
Political or communal perception Neutral tone, balanced source policy, no inflammatory headlines and transparent separation of reporting from opinion.
Translation changes legal meaning Human review by competent language/legal reviewers; preserve original statutory wording and official source link.
False government affiliation Clear independent-site disclaimer, original branding and no unauthorised emblem/seal on any page.
Sensitive data exposure Collect minimal data; avoid legal case intake; restrict staff access; define retention/deletion and incident response.
Admin compromise MFA where available, least privilege, rate limits, audit logs, secure deployment and recovery tests.
Content volume grows quickly Reusable content types, structured taxonomy, translation workflow and recurring review reminders.
